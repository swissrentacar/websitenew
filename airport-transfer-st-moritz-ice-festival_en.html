Plan your luggage in advance

Journey time & pickup

Around 3 hours from Zurich as a guide

St. Moritz Tourism gives a driving time of around three hours from Zurich. For your airport transfer, allow additional time for baggage collection and meeting your chauffeur. Snow, traffic and the route can extend the journey. We agree your pickup time individually.
