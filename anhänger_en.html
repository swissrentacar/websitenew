Trailer Rental FAQs

Dimensions, payload, equipment and booking – key answers about our enclosed trailer.

The payload is approximately 834 kg. The trailer has a permitted gross weight of 1,300 kg. Account for the weight of all items and secure your cargo before driving.

The internal dimensions are approximately 2,515 mm long, 1,330 mm wide and 1,685 mm high. For bulky items, also check the door opening before reserving.

It is an enclosed box trailer with sandwich side panels and double rear doors.

The trailer has a 13-pin connector and a reversing light. Before collection, check that your towing vehicle has a suitable connection.

This depends on your towing vehicle, the permitted weights and your driving entitlement. Check your vehicle and driving licence documents and confirm the combination before reserving.
