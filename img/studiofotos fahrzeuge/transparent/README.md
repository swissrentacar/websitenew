## Auto, Minivan und Minibus (Deutsch und Englisch)

Dateien: tucson.png, b-class.png, e-class.png, staria.png, v-class.png, vito.png, sprinter-13s.png, sprinter-16-19s.png, master-minibus.png.

Erstellt mit dem integrierten Imagegen-Tool. Prompt pro Originalfoto:
"Use case: background-extraction. Edit target: studio vehicle photo [original filename]. Remove ONLY the white studio backdrop, floor and cast shadow. Deliver a genuinely transparent PNG of this exact vehicle with crisp clean silhouette, zero white halo or fog, no background remnants. Preserve the existing vehicle model, body paint including white areas, badges, logos, license plate text, windows, mirrors, wheels, reflections, pose and camera angle. Preserve an open passenger door if present. Do not redesign or relight the vehicle. Keep the entire physical vehicle and antenna visible, uncropped, at its existing proportions, with a modest transparent margin. The vehicle stays opaque, only the surrounding backdrop becomes alpha zero."

Bei einzelnen Fotos bleibt ein heller Saum. CSS verwendet im zentrierten Slider zusätzlich multiply zur Anpassung an den hellen Seitenhintergrund.
