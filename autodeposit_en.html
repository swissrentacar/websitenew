Car Rental at Zurich Airport without a Credit Card

Arriving in Zurich without a credit card? Rent your car with Swiss Rent a Car in Kloten, near Zurich Airport, and explore Switzerland at your own pace.

Debit cards accepted: the CHF 1,000 security deposit can also be paid in cash.

Back to our cars · Book a car

No credit card? No problem.

Tourists can book a rental car without a credit card. Pay the CHF 1,000 security deposit by debit card or in cash. The deposit is separate from the rental price.

Your rental location near Zurich Airport

Our Kloten location is at Schaffhauserstrasse 137, 8302 Kloten, near Zurich Airport. Contact us before your arrival to arrange collection and discuss your booking.

How to book your car

Choose a vehicle from our fleet and book online. Let us know that you would like to pay the deposit by debit card or in cash, and bring your valid driving licence when collecting the car.
