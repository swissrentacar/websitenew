Vehicle rental without a credit card in Zurich

At Swiss Rent a Car in Kloten near Zurich Airport, you can rent a car, minivan, minibus or cargo van without a credit card. This also applies to tourists.

Pay the CHF 1,000 security deposit by debit card or in cash. The deposit is separate from the rental price.

A security deposit is only required if you do not reside in Switzerland or do not hold a Swiss passport.
