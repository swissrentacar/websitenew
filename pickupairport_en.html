How pickup at Zurich Airport works

We welcome you personally, complete the rental agreement on site and hand over your waiting rental vehicle.

View the process
Simple and personal

From landing directly to your rental car

To ensure a smooth pickup, we need your flight number when you book. This allows us to monitor your arrival and be ready for you at the right time.

01

Share your flight number

Provide your flight number when booking so that we can plan your arrival and pickup precisely.

02

Personal welcome

We wait for you in the arrivals hall with a name sign and warmly welcome you to Zurich Airport.

03

Agreement and handover

Your rental car is ready in the arrival area. We complete the rental agreement conveniently on site.

Meet & Greet

We wait with your name sign

After baggage claim, enter the arrivals hall. Our team member will be waiting there in a clearly visible position with your name sign.

  • Personal welcome in the arrivals hall
  • Direct route to your waiting vehicle
  • Rental agreement and vehicle handover on site

Important: Please send us your correct flight number in advance.
